## star hops
.graph
N Person Alice
N Person Bob
N Person Carol
E KNOWS Alice Bob
E KNOWS Bob Carol
.query
MATCH (a:Person {name:'Alice'})-[:KNOWS*1..2]->(b)
.expect
rows 2
a | b
Alice | Bob
Alice | Carol

## shortest hop
.graph
N City A
N City B
N City C
E ROAD A B

E ROAD A C
.query
MATCH p = shortestPath((x:City {name:'A'})-[:ROAD*]->(y:City {name:'C'})) RETURN p
.expect
rows 1
p
path:1
