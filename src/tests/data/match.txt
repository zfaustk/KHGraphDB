## one hop
.graph
N Person Alice
N Person Bob
N Person Carol
E KNOWS Alice Bob
E KNOWS Bob Carol
.query
MATCH (a:Person {name:'Alice'})-[:KNOWS]->(b)
.expect
rows 1
a | b
Alice | Bob
